Rakesh Sharma

Owner, second generation

My father, Mohan Lal Sharma, started this shop in 1998 with one kadhai and a wooden counter. I was 12 and my job was to wash the trays. I still remember the smell of ghee on the first Diwali, when the line reached the chai stall next door.

My chacha taught me the motichoor recipe and I have not changed it in 20 years. Many families here now buy from us for the third generation. That is the only award I care about.

Come in, taste first, and then decide. Nobody leaves without a piece to try.
